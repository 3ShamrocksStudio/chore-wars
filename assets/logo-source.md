# Chore Royale logo — source & direction

**Mark:** a glossy golden crown (royale) with a five-point star at its heart
(the ⭐ currency + game-show marquee light), on the royale-violet tile with a
radiating spotlight sunburst — game-show energy. Paired in-app with the Fredoka
"Chore Royale" wordmark (real type, never AI-rendered).

- Tool: xAI Grok (`grok-imagine-image-quality`) via `3s-design-system/tools/grok-asset.py`.
- Curated from 4 art-directed options; selected option 3 (spotlight-sunburst crown).
- Winning prompt (`--type icon`):
  > a premium mobile game app icon: a bold glossy golden crown with a single bright
  > five-point star set at its center, on a deep royale-violet rounded-square tile
  > with a subtle radial spotlight glow behind it, thick clean rounded vector shapes,
  > vibrant and cheerful game-show energy, crisp edges, perfectly centered with
  > balanced padding, no text, no letters, no words
  > brand: Chore Royale — royale violet #7a30dc + game-show gold #f59e0b; bold, rounded, glossy, premium
- Applied: app icon/favicon, header mark, onboarding + who hero lockup, wheel-hub center.
