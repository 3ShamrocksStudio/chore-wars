Every parent knows this conversation.

It starts the same way every time. You ask. They say "in a minute." A minute becomes twenty. You ask again. It becomes an argument. By the time the chore is done, everyone's exhausted and nobody feels good about it — not you, not them.

כל הורה מכיר את השיחה הזאת. היא מתחילה אותו הדבר כל פעם.

What if the chore wasn't your idea?

Kids don't argue with a wheel. They can't negotiate with randomness. When the spin lands on "Take out the trash," it's not mom or dad's fault — it's the game. And somehow, that changes everything.

ילדים לא מתווכחים עם גלגל. הם לא יכולים לנהל משא ומתן עם האקראיות.

The chore becomes a quest.

They spin. They get their mission. They do it, take a photo to prove it, and earn XP. Real XP — that shows on the leaderboard, that builds their warrior, that compounds into streaks and boss raids. The pride isn't in doing the chore. It's in proving they did it.

הם מסתובבים. מקבלים את המשימה. עושים אותה, מצלמים הוכחה, ומרוויחים XP.
